How an engagement runs

Small, fixed-price steps. You see what each one delivers before you agree to the next.

  1. Discovery call

    30 minutes, free

    We learn how your week runs, where the time goes and who decides. You leave with a straight answer on whether we can help.

  2. Diagnostic

    1 to 2 weeks, fixed fee

    We map two to four of your processes, measure the hours and errors, and hand you a roadmap showing what each automation would save. The fee comes off your first build.

  3. Pilot build

    2 to 4 weeks, fixed price

    We build one automation against one target agreed up front, such as every enquiry answered within five minutes, and measure it before and after.

  4. Ongoing care

    Monthly

    We host and monitor it, fix it when a supplier changes something, keep the AI current and improve it. Then we take the next item off your roadmap.
